Missing Content-Security-Policy Medium Severity

Enforce a strict CSP header to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) and unauthorized script injection.

Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; script-src 'self'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'self';
HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) Low Severity

Force modern browsers to always communicate via encrypted HTTPS to stop SSL stripping.

Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains; preload

Executive Security Assessment

Target: example.com • Date: November 8, 2026

1. Executive Summary

DataSecurely completed an automated, safe external security assessment of example.com. The site achieved an overall security rating of Grade A (92/100). No critical or high vulnerabilities were detected. Two configuration improvements were identified regarding browser security headers and session cookie flags.

2. Business & Regulatory Impact (India DPDP Act 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023 and CERT-In compliance mandates, websites handling Indian citizen data are obligated to implement standard transport and boundary defenses. Enforcing HSTS and CSP ensures adherence with basic cybersecurity hygiene requirements.

3. Recommended Action Plan

  • Deploy Content-Security-Policy header to whitelist sanctioned origins.
  • Enable Strict-Transport-Security with a one-year expiry (31536000 seconds).
  • Ensure authentication session cookies carry the SameSite=Lax and Secure attributes.
